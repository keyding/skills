## `ab-testing`

**Skill overview:** Designs statistically sound experiments, including hypotheses, metrics, sample sizing, duration, and result interpretation.

**When and how to use:** Invoke `/ab-testing` when comparing variants in a product, page, offer, or campaign. Provide the proposed change, primary metric, traffic volume, and decision deadline so it can produce a test plan and guard against premature conclusions.
